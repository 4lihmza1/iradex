Built for the moment motivation disappears

Progress, not perfection.

Iradex does not demand that you finish everything. It asks you to begin one meaningful next step—and leave evidence that you moved.

01

Commit

Choose one small, specific task and the time Iradex should call you back.

02

Act

The alarm rings even when the app is closed and recovers after a phone restart.

03

Prove

Add a fresh photo or quick note. Honest progress stops the alarm and builds momentum.

A quieter kind of accountability

Designed to feel focused—not punishing.

The experience is deliberately simple: one commitment, a clear moment, and a safe way back to action.

Private by default

Your progress stays yours.

The alpha has no accounts, cloud database, advertising SDK or analytics tracker. Commitments, history and proof remain in private storage on your Android phone.
