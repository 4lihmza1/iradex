# Iradex Alpha Privacy Policy

Effective 5 August 2026 · Version 0.3.1-alpha

Iradex has no account system, advertising, cloud database or analytics tracker. The alpha stores active commitments, onboarding preferences, completion history, progress notes and proof photos in private local Android storage. A minimal device-protected copy of an active alarm is used only to restore it after a phone restart.

The app requests notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts, camera, vibration, wake-lock and boot-completed access for its commitment-alarm workflow. These permissions can be revoked in Android Settings, although doing so may prevent alarms from working reliably.

The alpha does not sell or upload personal data. Testers choose what to share through GitHub issues, messages or surveys and should never post private proof or sensitive information publicly.

Users can remove local data by clearing Iradex storage or uninstalling the app. There is no cloud backup.

Iradex is a productivity prototype, not a medical device, ADHD treatment, emergency alarm or substitute for professional care. Every alarm includes an Emergency stop.

The complete policy is published on the Iradex website.
