# Iradex v0.3.1 Alpha Tester Guide

## Before testing

- Android 8.0 or newer
- Notifications, Exact alarm and Full-screen alert must show **Ready**
- Do not use Iradex as a medical or emergency alarm

## Core test

1. Create one small commitment for 3–5 minutes ahead.
2. Choose photo proof or a quick progress note.
3. Lock the phone and wait without reopening Iradex.
4. When it rings, submit honest progress or use Emergency stop.
5. Check Progress to confirm the completion was recorded.

## Expected behavior

- Locked phone: sound, vibration and full-screen alarm.
- Another app open: sound and notification; tapping it opens Iradex.
- Removed from Recents: alarm still rings.
- Restarted phone: alarm is restored without reopening Iradex.
- Emergency stop: alarm ends without recording proof.

## Known limitations

- Photo capture is recorded, but the image is not displayed in history.
- Android Force stop disables alarms until Iradex is opened again.
- Manufacturer battery restrictions may change alarm behavior.
- No accounts, cloud sync, backup or automatic updates.

Submit feedback using the repository's **Beta feedback** issue form. Do not include private proof or sensitive information.
